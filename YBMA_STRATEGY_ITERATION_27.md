# What was produced

**1 · Master artwork package** (`identity/logo/`) — regenerated from the locked geometry by
`logo/master/export.js`; nothing hand-drawn. Wordmark, symbol, horizontal lockup, stacked lockup ×
ink / indigo / white × SVG / PDF (vector, arcs converted to Béziers, verified through a second
renderer) / PNG (transparent, three sizes). **60 files** + MANIFEST. Geometry preserved exactly:
stroke 16 · radius 21 · tracking 12 · 1.2 crown correction · square terminals · U3 unchanged · clear
space and minimum sizes unchanged. White versions live in `logo/reversed/`; no new variations.

**2 · File structure** — `identity/{logo/{master,wordmark,symbol,lockups,reversed}, brand/{colour,
typography,icons,web}, templates, guidelines, archive}` with `identity/README.md` documenting masters
vs exports and the naming convention `ybma-<asset>-<colour>[-<size>].<ext>`.

**3 · Tokens** (`brand/colour/tokens.css` + `.json`) — three explicit groups: **brand** (Indigo, Ink,
Chalk) · **supporting / UI** (White, Mist, Clay, greys, Indigo 60) · **semantic product status** (OK,
warning, error, info — UI kit only). Type, spacing, grid, shape, logo and motion tokens; consistent
with the guideline.

**4 · Söhne** — Buch · Kräftig · Halbfett · Mono Buch · Breit Kräftig (one cover word). Archivo
withdrawn entirely. Licensing documented as an operational note (`brand/typography/TYPOGRAPHY.md`).

**5 · Component library** (`brand/web/ybma.css` + `components.html`) — logo, symbol, lockups, label,
display, heading, body, proof block, inverted field, one-curved-end panels, image crops and the cut,
stems/rules, buttons, links, bars, division descriptor, endorsement, navigation, product status.
Every component obeys one curved end / one square end and one round thing per composition.

**6 · Stress test** — ten situations from one system: website hero · company profile · proposal
cover · presentation cover · social post · YBMA Technology header · Technology dashboard · Real
Estate · Agriculture · Financial Services. Coherent throughout; no division colours.

**7 · Production specification** (`guidelines/PRODUCTION.md`) — indigo #1F2C6B with **Pantone 2758 C
as a provisional direction until a physical swatch is signed**; coated/uncoated (2758 U / 2748 U to
proof); ink as K100 / rich black; reversed rules; stamp, embroidery, vinyl and signage floors derived
from the geometry. **Desk-test result: no mark fails at its guideline minimum; the symbol's joint is
the tightest feature and sets the stamp (15 mm) and embroidery (25 mm) floors. Nothing calls for a
change to the geometry — the rule is size, or the other mark.**

**8 · Web foundation** (`brand/web/WEB_FOUNDATION.md`) — tokens, type, spacing, 12-column grid,
layout rules, buttons, links, image treatment, panels, responsive behaviour, logo usage, navigation,
accessibility. The website is an implementation of this, not an extension.

**9 · Guideline v1.0** — twenty sections: essence · strategic foundations · logo · symbol ·
construction · variations · clear space · minimum sizes · incorrect usage · colour · typography ·
visual language · photography · iconography · architecture · endorsement · applications · digital
implementation · production specifications · do/don't — plus the record of why there is no accent.
