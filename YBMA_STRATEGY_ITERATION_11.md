# A · The construction

**The problem set last session:** four letters in a box read as four letters in a box.

**The solution, in one decision:**

> **The Y's stem is the only thing in the mark that crosses the datum.**

One deliberate event, on the letter that carries the name. Everything else meets the line cleanly.
The negative space becomes **one connected field** — datum edge-to-edge, plus the stem descending
through it into the M's opening. That single asymmetry is what stops the mark reading as a tidy 2×2
grid, and it is what the eye holds at small size.

**Grid:** 120 units · stem 10 · diagonals 8 · crossbar 7 · datum 6 · cap height 40.

---

# B · Letterform decisions

| Question | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Y junction height** | **45% of cap height** | The most characterful decision available. Low junction = long stem = **the crossing becomes possible.** Higher and the mark loses its one distinctive event |
| **Y / A diagonals** | Y opens 38°, A closes 36° | Near-mirrored across the datum. Deliberately *not* identical — identical reads mechanical |
| **B bowls** | Flattened to chamfers; upper counter set 1u higher | No curves anywhere in the mark. Mathematically equal bowls always look top-heavy |
| **M width** | Same 40u cell as Y | The grid does the narrowing. A natural-width M blows the left column open. V stops at 78% — opens the letter, keeps the mark off its heels |
| **A apex** | **Flat, 14u wide** | A pointed A would be the only sharp point in the mark. The flat top answers the M's flat V and the B's chamfers |
| **Negative space** | **One connected field** | Datum + crossing merge the two rows. Only three closed counters in the whole mark |
| **Corner geometry** | Chamfers on B only | Two exceptions in the entire mark: one softening, one crossing |
| **Stroke consistency** | Stems 10 · diagonals 8 · crossbar 7 | Diagonals and horizontals must be lighter than verticals or they read heavier |
| **Mechanical or optical?** | **Constructed mechanically, corrected optically** | Pure mechanical construction is exactly what a 1970s conglomerate looks like. **The corrections are what make it contemporary** |

# C · The datum, tested

Six versions built. The brief said remove it if it doesn't earn its place.

| | Version | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **D1** | Through + crossing | **SELECTED.** Strongest silhouette; the crossing survives to 32px |
| D2 | Through, no crossing | **Rejected — this is the 1970s conglomerate.** Two clean bars, tidy and symmetrical |
| D3 | External tick | **Cut from the mark.** Breaks the square, ruins the favicon, disappears below 64px. **Retained in the wider system as a rule, not on the mark** |
| D4 | Offset / partial | Rejected — reads as an error, not a decision |
| D5 | Thick datum | Rejected — splits the mark into two logos stacked |
| D6 | **No datum (control)** | **Rejected — and this is the proof.** Four letters in a box. Generic, and it collapses below 48px. **The datum earns its place** |

# D · Small size — the honest finding

512, 128 and 64 hold fully. **32 is the floor for the full mark.**

> **Four letters cannot be read at 16px.** Any four-letter block has this problem and it would be
> dishonest to claim otherwise. At 16px the mark becomes a *pattern* — dark square, light band, one
> notch — recognisable once learned, but by silhouette rather than by reading.

**The fix is a 16px variant, not a compromise to the main mark:** the datum and the crossing alone,
letters dropped. The same object reduced to its one distinctive event — consistent, not a second
logo.

---

# E · Wordmark

Drawn from the same letterforms as the mark — same Y junction, same flat A apex, same chamfered B,
same narrowed M. **The wordmark is the mark unstacked.**

**Lockup:** mark height = wordmark cap height. Gap = one stem width. No tick, no rule between them.

# F · Endorsement system

| Wording | Verdict |
|---|---|
| **A YBMA COMPANY** | **SELECTED.** 14 characters, one line at any width. Says ownership without claiming operational credit YBMA has not yet earned |
| BUILT AND OPERATED BY YBMA | Rejected — forces two lines, becomes a block rather than a line of record. And **"built" is false on an acquisition YBMA bought rather than made** |
| OWNED AND OPERATED BY YBMA | Rejected as a lockup — accurate but too long. **Better as the brand essence than as a mark.** Keep it in language |

**Divisions:** identical mark, weight and position. Only the descriptor changes — data typeface,
below a rule. **Acquisitions:** the acquired brand keeps primacy and gains a line of record beneath.

# G · Colour — refined, not replaced

Two controlled adjustments, both for reasons the brief raised.

| | Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Graphite** | `#1B1F1D` | **`#1C1B18`** | The old value leaned green and read as slate — cold. Rotated to a brown-black it reads as **iron and ink**, and it gives the paper relationship the warmth asked about |
| **Signal** | `#CE3B1F` | **`#BF3A22`** | `#CE3B1F` sat close to **hi-vis safety orange** — the construction cliché to avoid. `#BF3A22` is an **enamel red**: still a machine red, no longer a warning triangle |
| **Slate** | — | **`#4A4A44` added** | Financial-services gravity **without inventing a second palette** |

| Colour | Hex · RGB | CMYK approx | Role | Contrast on Paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Graphite | `#1C1B18` · 28 27 24 | 0 4 14 89 | Primary — text, mark, grounds | ≈16.3:1 **AAA** |
| Signal | `#BF3A22` · 191 58 34 | 0 70 82 25 | Accent only. **Never a field** | ≈5.0:1 **AA all sizes** |
| Slate | `#4A4A44` · 74 74 68 | 0 0 8 71 | Financial gravity | ≈8.6:1 **AAA** |
| Zinc | `#8E8B82` · 142 139 130 | 0 2 8 44 | Annotation, inactive | ≈3.3:1 — large/UI only |
| Bone | `#E7E4DC` · 231 228 220 | 0 1 5 9 | Surfaces | ground |
| Paper | `#F3F1EC` · 243 241 236 | 0 1 3 5 | Primary ground | ground |

**Signal on Graphite ≈3.1:1** — display and marks only, never body text.
**Monochrome:** the whole system collapses to Graphite on Paper with nothing lost — which is how the
endorsement will mostly live.

**Sector fitness:** Financial services — Signal is too hot at volume, **resolved by Slate**;
financial material runs Graphite/Slate/Paper with Signal reduced to a rule. Property — strong, reads
as site marking. Agriculture — strong, and **better than green**, which would bring the produce
cliché. Technology — strong; red on warm paper is the furthest thing from a SaaS palette.

# H · Typography

**Verdict: stay with Monument Grotesk.** Tested against Basis Grotesque as asked.

Basis has more idiosyncrasy and less authority. This identity has to hold a financial-services
business inside five years, and **quirk in the typeface is the first thing that reads as a startup
trying to look bigger than it is.**

> **The youth should come from weight contrast, tight display tracking and unstyled photography —
> never from the face.** A characterful typeface would be a second personality in a system that
> already has a strong mark, and two personalities is one too many.

Open-source until licensed: **Archivo + IBM Plex Mono.** Tabular figures throughout.

# J · Attack — and three weaknesses I won't paper over

**Clears:** construction company · bank · generic African conglomerate · SaaS startup · consultancy ·
startup-pretending-to-be-bigger · survives 20 years · survives all four founders leaving.

**The three that don't fully clear:**

**1 · It cannot be read at 16px.** Inherent to any four-letter block. **Mitigated by the reduced
variant, not solved.**

**2 · It is masculine.** Heavy, square, industrial. **The mark will not fix this** — the photography,
the writing and the colour restraint have to, and that is an ongoing obligation rather than a
one-time decision.

**3 · The 2×2 is not distinctive on its own.** A stacked block is a known construction.
**Everything rests on the datum and the crossing.** That is a narrow foundation, and it means the
execution of one detail carries the identity.

**And one rule that must hold or the red does too much:** **Signal is a mark, a rule or a state —
never a field.**

# K · Recommendation

> **D1 — the block with the datum and the single crossing.**
> Colour **C1 refined** (Graphite `#1C1B18` · Signal `#BF3A22` · Slate `#4A4A44`).
> Type **Monument Grotesk + Mono**. Endorsement **A YBMA COMPANY**.

**What this is not.** A construction, not a finished logo. Before production it needs: letterforms
redrawn at high resolution with overshoot and ink-trap decisions · a full clear-space and
minimum-size specification · **and physical proofing — a mark this reliant on one 6-unit line must
be tested on paper and on metal, not only on screen.**
