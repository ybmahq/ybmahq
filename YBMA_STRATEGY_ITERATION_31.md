  system) → who it is for (any kind of business that has outgrown the way it works). No industry,
  workflow or software category named as a market; the inventory/distribution/clinic examples are
  gone. Home, company and contact copy aligned.

  for a static site with no cookies or analytics; `hello@ybmahq.com` as the contact; counsel-review
