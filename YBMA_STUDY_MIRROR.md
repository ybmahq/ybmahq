# YBMA — Side Study: Mirror

**Exploration only · not a direction · 14 September 2026**
**Visual:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/92298032-0841-475c-a5bb-30987259383e

> Founder request, after the Turned + U3 lock recommendation: *"Let's play around with something —
> a different one, not a replacement. Y should mirror A, and M and B should mirror each other: A
> becomes an upside-down Y, B an upright M. Do the curves and lines properly so it gets the point
> across and looks sleek."*

> Second pass, same day: *"The Y and A look inverted and the A won't be clear enough. Make the Y's
> bridges curved — umbrella-like — and the A an upside-down umbrella; a dot or something to signify
> each. Get the point across that it's a Y and unmistakably an A; the curves are so it matches the
> roundness of B and M."*

## Construction (second pass — drawn)

**One arch, four letters.** Monoline, stroke 16 on cap 100, round joins, **one radius (21)**,
tracking 12, width 315.

| | Made from the arch | Width |
|---|---|---|
| **Y** | open upward, on a stem — the cup; straight sides 24 before the curve | 58 |
| **B** | two arches lying on a stem, waist at 50 | 63 |
| **M** | two arches standing on legs | 100 |
| **A** | one arch standing on legs, with a bar at 66 | 58 |

The Y and the A are the same arch flipped; **the stem and the bar are what make each unmistakable.**
The dot version was drawn as asked and rejected on sight: a cup with a dot below reads as a face, an
arch with a dot above as an n with a ring — a dot says "something is here" without saying which letter.

### First pass — superseded

Y ↔ A by literal 180° rotation (barless A, mast 36) and B ↔ M by a quarter turn of the arches. The
A read as an inverted Y, as the founders said. Kept in the record only.

## Read

**Does well:** one radius everywhere — the B's bowls, the M's shoulders, the Y's cup and the A's crown
are the same curve, and you see it before you are told; every letter is now legible; sleek; it sits
closer in spirit to U3 (two pieces, one angle) than either Cut or Turned.

**Costs:** the M is a lowercase *m* (and the A carries a faint lowercase-n ancestry, held by its bar);
**the register is soft** — rounded monoline reads consumer, and YBMA has to carry property and
finance; the Y and A are narrow (58) against a 100-wide M, so the word is heavy in the middle —
tunable, not tuned.

**Kept in the drawer:** one radius for four letters (the best B the project has drawn), and the
monoline-round-join drawing style.

**Status:** side study. **Turned + U3 remains the identity.** Nothing here is proposed.

## Branch B — the rocket A

**Visual:** https://claude.ai/code/artifact/1f74ac3b-0ef4-4a28-9540-dbb14237220f · branch A above is unchanged.

> *"Continue with what we have except the A — it still looks too basic. A rounded top and a line
> that completes it below: think of a triangle, then space, then a line — but cooler, flowing with
> the rest of the letters' curves."*

**The A (revised the same day — "still doesn't look like an A; rounder, and a dot inside"):** a dome
of **radius 28** — the one letter allowed to be rounder than the system's 21, because it is the event
— legs splayed 8°, stopping at 72 · a gap of one stroke · a base line at the baseline as wide as the
feet · **a dot (one stroke wide) at 60, where an A keeps its counter** · width 83. The dot makes it an
A rather than a rocket; the gap makes it a rocket rather than an A. Two pieces, one gap — **U3's
construction (two masses, one joint) in a letter.**

*First rocket (superseded):* radius 21, legs 12°, no dot — read as a Λ over a line, not an A.

Studied and shown: first rocket / rounder without dot / **rounder with dot** · radius 21 (nose cone)
/ **28** (dome) / bell (legs curve too — a dome tent) · base full-width / inset (launch pad) / joined
(the gap was the design).

**Read:** the first letter in the project with a visible event that is not a trick; the closest the
wordmark and U3 have come to one construction. **Costs:** the M is still a lowercase *m*; the register
is still soft; the A (76) is now wide against a narrow Y (58); and **the gap is a production risk of
the class that disqualified Unclosed** — the dot and the gap hold at 20 px and both close at 14 (the A
becomes a solid rounded triangle over a line — still an A); untested under ink gain. Status unchanged: side study; Turned + U3 remains the identity.
