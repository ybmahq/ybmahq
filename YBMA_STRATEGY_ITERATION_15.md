# The mechanism is fixed

> **A piece is taken from one side of a mass. The same piece appears on another side.**
> Same size, same shape. **Nothing created, nothing lost.**

That is not a variable. **Six things inside it are:**

| Lever | Range |
|---|---|
| **Unit size** | How big the moved piece is relative to the mass |
| **Offset** | How far apart the notch and the tab sit |
| **Mass proportion** | Square · portrait · landscape |
| **Position** | Mid-edge or corner |
| **Axis** | Left/right or top/bottom |
| **Contact** | Attached or moved clear |

**The problem being solved:** the baseline reads a little like a battery — and a battery is
precisely *a rectangular mass with a small nub on one mid-edge*. **So the escape routes are inside
the levers: make the unit bigger, move it to a corner, or change the mass proportion.** All three
keep T4 intact.

# Recommendation — V7 · corner to corner, large unit

**The mechanism is untouched.** A square piece taken from one corner of the mass, appearing at the
opposite corner. Same size, same shape.

**Two changes do the work:**

1. **Moving both events to corners kills the battery and key reads outright** — a battery terminal
   is always mid-edge, and a key's cuts are always on one flank.
2. **Enlarging the unit** (30 on a 72 mass) makes it a *substantial displacement* rather than a nub
   — which is what made the baseline feel incidental.

**It also picks up a diagonal without adding one.** Top-left to bottom-right is implied by the two
corners, giving movement the earlier orthogonal versions never had. **And it survives to 16px,
because corners blur last.**

## What I would still watch

**A faint S.** Corner-to-corner displacement traces a shallow S through the form. Invisible at small
size; arguable at large. Worth checking with fresh eyes.

**The ratio is not final.** Mass 72 to unit 30 is a starting point, not a resolved proportion. Next
round should test **1:2, 12:5 and 1:3** side by side at real sizes before anything is fixed.

## Rejected inside T4

**V5 moved-clear** — becomes two objects · **V6 vertical axis** — plug or folder tab ·
**V3 portrait** — door or card · **V1 baseline** — the battery read.
