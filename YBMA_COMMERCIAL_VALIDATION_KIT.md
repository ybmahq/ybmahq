# PART 2 · CONVERSATION SCRIPT

**30–45 minutes.** Business ____________ Date ________ Present: ______________________

## THE THREE RULES — read before you go in

**1. They state a number before you state a price.** Stage 4 before Stage 7. Always.

**2. If no margin or reconciliation problem emerges *unprompted* at Stage 3 — STOP SELLING.**
Finish politely, learn what their real problem is, mark **FAILURE** on the score sheet.
*Educating them into the problem produces a false positive and destroys the experiment.*

**3. Every conversation ends with an ask for money.** Not for agreement.

**Never say:** digital transformation · solutions · platform · partner · infrastructure ·
transformation · leverage · seamless.
