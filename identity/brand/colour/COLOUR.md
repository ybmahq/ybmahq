# YBMA colour · CONFIRMED (Session 27)

**Brand colours — three.** Nothing else is a brand colour.

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (ref., proof before print) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **YBMA Indigo** | #1F2C6B | 31 44 107 | C71 M59 Y0 K58 · Pantone direction 2758 C (provisional) | A full field, or the one emphasised element on chalk. Never a tint, never twice on one surface, never on ink |
| **Ink** | #151412 | 21 20 18 | rich black C60 M50 Y40 K100 | All text, the logo on light grounds, rules, icons, buttons. Also the night ground |
| **Chalk** | #F5F4F0 | 245 244 240 | paper — C0 M0 Y2 K4 if printed | The default ground; type on indigo and ink |

**Supporting / UI colours — functional, not brand.** White #FFFFFF · Mist #E3E6F3 · Clay #E9DCCF
(print only) · Indigo 60 #3446A8 (product UI only: focus, selected, hover) · Greys 700 #4B4A46 ·
500 #7A7873 · 300 #B8B6AF · 200 #DAD8D2 · 100 #ECEAE5.

**Semantic product status — YBMA Technology UI kit only, never in marketing.** OK #2E6B4B ·
Warning #8C6A1F (deliberately not amber) · Error #A33A2E · Info = Indigo 60. Indicators only (dot,
badge, border, icon); never a fill behind a text block.

**Rules.** Proportion on a chalk surface 64 · 20 · 10 · 6 (chalk · ink · indigo · greys); an indigo
surface is indigo 80 · white 20. Surfaces alternate chalk and indigo. Indigo and ink never meet.
Emphasis comes from scale, the field, the curve, weight and photography — never from a hue.

**Contrast.** White on Indigo 12.8 · Ink on Chalk 16.7 · Indigo on Chalk 12.0 · Indigo 60 on White
8.4 · Chalk on Ink 16.0 — all AAA. Indigo on Ink 1.5 — never.
