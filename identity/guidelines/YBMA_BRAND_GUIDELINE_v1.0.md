## 1 · Brand essence

**YBMA builds and runs businesses properly — and builds them to do more than they had to.**

- **The Operator** (settled strategic direction): hands-on, responsible for how things run
  afterwards; not an investor at a distance, not an agency that leaves.
- **Long-term idea (recorded):** *in ten years, YBMA should mean it was built to do more than it
  had to.*
- **Origin (recorded):** Nigerian by authorship, global by standard — *built for the hardest
  conditions, therefore good enough for the easiest.* Stated once, never explained, never a motif.
- **Fixed facts:** the name is spelled, never spoken · four is never counted or illustrated · the
  parent is independent of the technology business · plain speech.

## 2 · Strategic foundations

**Locked, not reinterpreted:** the Operator · Nigerian by authorship, global by standard · the parent
independent of YBMA Technology · plain speech · young + serious · professional + human · one identity
across future businesses.

### Strategy
One parent identity, carried by standards, that ten unrelated businesses can share without a
rebrand. Now: win digital-services work in Nigeria from buyers burned by technology they could not
keep using. Later: carry a diversified group and endorse acquired businesses without making each one
a sub-brand. The identity answers both with a compact sector-neutral wordmark, a symbol for where the
wordmark cannot go, a palette that is neither "tech" nor "property" nor "finance", and a visual
language built on one rule.

**YBMA Technology's recorded direction:** *operational clarity — we build the systems that show you
what's actually happening in your business.* Proof points, not claims: client owns domain, code and
accounts from day one · named handover artefact · fixed support window with a date · built for
Nigerian conditions.

### Positioning
Nigerian → African → global. Professional, not boring. Young, not childish. Not a 1985 bank, not a
software startup, not a property company, not a consultancy, not "African premium", not
generic-African.

| Axis | YBMA | Not |
|---|---|---|
| Weight | Confident, medium-heavy | Ultra-bold conglomerate · hairline luxury |
| Warmth | Round mark, hard system | All-round friendly · all-square cold |
| Colour | Deep indigo, ink and chalk — nothing else | Corporate blue · Nigerian green · startup gradient · a bright accent |
| Voice | Plain, first person plural | Slogans · jargon · "empowering" |
| Image | Documentary, people working | Stock boardrooms · lifestyle Africa |

### Personality — rules that hold the tensions
**Young + serious (not playful):** energy from scale, not colour — there is no accent to lean on; a
flat surface gets a bigger number, a harder crop or an inverted field, never a hue · one round thing
per surface · emphasis is a field, not a colour · sentence-case headlines · cuts and quarter-turns,
never bounce.
**Professional + human (not boring):** people doing things in every hero · first person plural, plain
speech · show the number (proof is the brand's warmth) · ink, not grey · asymmetry.

## 3 · The logo

**One arch, four letters.** A single semicircle (radius 21 on a stroke of 16, cap 100) lies twice to
make the B, stands twice to make the M, opens upward on a stem to make the Y, and closes over a bar
to make the A. Every terminal is square. 315 × 100. Tracking 12.

**Recognisably YBMA because:** one curve used four ways (describable in a sentence) · a skyline
silhouette (narrow–wide–narrow) · square terminals (a drawn mark, not a rounded font) · compact.

**How it expresses the Operator:** made the way YBMA says it makes things — one module, understood
completely, used consistently, nothing added for effect. Roundness is the human side; the square
terminals and single radius are the seriousness.

**Never change:** stroke 16 · radius 21 · the A's bar at 58–74 · the Y's cup depth (24) · square
terminals · tracking 12 · the letter order and case · single colour. Never set in a typeface.

## 4 · The symbol

**U3 — "two stones, one joint" — CONFIRMED, carried forward unchanged.** 120 square · the joint enters
the top edge at 18 · descends at dx/dy 0.5625 · turns square at 86 · joint 11 · lower-right corner
sheared across the band. Two solid masses made one by a joint: the group in miniature.

**Related to the wordmark by construction philosophy, not geometry** — the guideline claims no
derivation. **Division of labour:** the wordmark by default; the symbol where the wordmark cannot go
(favicon, app icon, avatar, stamp, signet, the endorsement mark); the lockup where the symbol is being
introduced beside the name.

**Never change:** the angle, the joint (11) and the sheared corner, cut across the full band and never
deeper · the entry at 18 · single colour · never outlined, never decoration or pattern, never beside
the wordmark except in the two lockups.

## 5 · Logo construction

Production geometry outlines the approved strokes exactly (outer radius 29, inner 13; every curve is
tangent to its straight). Verified by overlay against the approved drawing. **One correction:** the M
and A crowns overshoot the cap by 1.2 units so round tops read level with flat tops. Monoline weight
deliberately uncorrected. Source of truth: `identity/logo/master/ybma-logo.js`.

| Y | sides 0→24, semicircle below (stroke bottom at 53), stem to baseline | 58 | 0 |
| B | stem; two equal bowls (bars at 8 and 92); shared waist bar at 50 | 63 | 70 |

## 6 · Logo variations (five; nothing else made)

1. **Wordmark** — primary, everywhere it fits.
2. **Horizontal lockup** — symbol at cap height, 0.4 cap from the wordmark; for introducing the
   symbol beside the name (signage, document headers, launch material, the first year of a channel).
3. **Symbol** — favicon, app icon, avatar, stamp, signet, endorsement mark.
4. **Stacked lockup** — square formats and splash screens only.
5. **Reversed** — white on ink or indigo; the same artwork.

Colours the logo appears in: ink · white · indigo (on chalk or white). White on ink is the night
ground (signage, avatars, workwear); indigo never sits on ink. **No small-size variant** — the
wordmark holds to 20 px; the symbol takes over below.

## 7 · Clear space

Wordmark and lockups: **X = 32 units (two strokes, ⅓ cap)** on all sides. Symbol: **X = 24 (one fifth
of the side)**. Minimum, not target; nothing enters the clear space.

## 8 · Minimum sizes

Wordmark **20 px / 6 mm** · symbol **16 px / 5 mm** · lockup **24 px / 7 mm**, by height. Physical
process floors are higher — see §19.

## 9 · Incorrect usage

Any of these changes the identity: stretched or squashed · outlined · gradient or two colours · drop
shadow or effects · rotated · set in a typeface · any single letter alone · descriptor in another
colour or inside the clear space · the symbol as decoration or pattern · indigo on ink.

## 10 · Colour — CONFIRMED

**Three colours. Ink does the work, chalk is the ground, indigo is the field. Colour comes from the
world, not from the palette.**

**Three groups, and only the first is brand:** *brand* — Indigo, Ink, Chalk · *supporting / UI* —
White, Mist, Clay, the greys, Indigo 60 (functional; never promoted) · *semantic product status* —
OK, warning, error, info (YBMA Technology UI kit only). Tokens: `identity/brand/colour/tokens.css` /
`.json`.

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (ref., proof before print) | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **YBMA Indigo** | #1F2C6B | 31 44 107 | C71 M59 Y0 K58 | **Two uses only:** a full field (cover, hero panel, fascia, slide, card back) or the one emphasised element on chalk (a number, a word, a panel, the key bar). The logo on light grounds where ink is too plain |
| **Ink** | #151412 | 21 20 18 | rich black C60 M50 Y40 K100 | All text · the logo on light grounds · rules, icons, buttons, UI chrome · **also a ground** — the night surface |
| **Chalk** | #F5F4F0 | 245 244 240 | C0 M0 Y2 K4 | The default ground, ≥ 60% of any light surface · type on indigo and ink |
| White | #FFFFFF | | | Digital surfaces, product UI, the logo on indigo and ink |
| Indigo 60 | #3446A8 | 52 70 168 | | **Product UI only:** focus rings, selected states, link hover |
| Mist | #E3E6F3 | | | Quiet indigo tint for panels and table stripes |
| Clay | #E9DCCF | | | Quiet warm tint, print only, never darker |
| Grey 700 / 500 / 300 / 200 / 100 | #4B4A46 · #7A7873 · #B8B6AF · #DAD8D2 · #ECEAE5 | | | Secondary text · captions · disabled · rules · UI surfaces |
| Status OK / Warning / Error / Info | #2E6B4B · #8C6A1F · #A33A2E · #3446A8 | | | **UI kit only** — indicators, never fills, never in marketing |

**How Indigo and Ink divide the work**
- **Ink** is the working colour: every word, the logo, every rule and icon, every button (ink fill,
  chalk text on light grounds; chalk fill, ink text on dark). Ink is a legitimate major ground — the
  "night" surface — carrying chalk type and the white logo.
- **Indigo** is a field or an element, never a tint. On a chalk surface it appears **once**: one
  panel, one number, one word, one bar — or as the logo. On its own surface it is total: indigo 80,
  white 20. **Indigo does not dominate because it is the primary; it dominates a surface or is absent
  from it.**
- **Chalk** appears on indigo as type (white for the logo); indigo appears on chalk as the one
  element; **indigo and ink never meet** — contrast 1.5. They sit on one page only with chalk between.
- **Proportion on a chalk surface:** chalk 64 · ink 20 · indigo 10 · greys 6. Pages, slides and posts
  **alternate** chalk and indigo grounds — the turn, in colour.

**Emphasis without an accent — five devices, none of them a hue**
1. **Scale** — the proof number or the sentence is the largest thing on the surface.
2. **The field** — one inverted panel (indigo or ink) per surface, carrying the one thing that matters.
3. **The curve** — the composition's single round element is the emphasised one (in a chart, the key
   bar is the only one with a round end; the rest are grey and square).
4. **Weight and the stem** — Halbfett against Buch; a 2 px ink rule beside the line. Links are
   underlined ink; hover indigo.
5. **Photography** — with no accent in the palette, the hero image is where colour enters.

**Why indigo:** Nigeria's dye colour (Kano, Abeokuta) and the world's denim — origin without motif;
deep enough for finance and property, violet enough not to be a bank. **Why no accent:** an accent is
the fastest way to look like every other young company; withholding one forces the energy into the
things that are YBMA's. In a category where everyone has a bright, three colours with discipline is
the rarer look.

**Contrast (WCAG):** white on Indigo 12.8 (AAA) · Ink on Chalk 16.7 (AAA) · Indigo on Chalk 12.0
(AAA) · Indigo 60 on white 8.4 (AAA) · Chalk on Ink 16.0 (AAA) · **Indigo on Ink 1.5 — never.**

## 11 · Typography — CONFIRMED: SÖHNE

**YBMA uses Söhne** (Klim Type Foundry). One family, six roles; no second display face; no
substitute inside the brand system.

| Role | Cut · setting | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Display | **Söhne Halbfett** · 44–96 · −3% · 0.98 | Sentence case, one idea, never all caps |
| Heading | **Söhne Halbfett** · 24–40 · −2% · 1.1 | Left-aligned, balanced wrap |
| Label | **Söhne Kräftig** · 11–13 · +14% · caps | Eyebrows, section marks, endorsement, descriptors — ink or grey, never indigo |
| Body | **Söhne Buch** · 16–18 · 1.5 | ≤ 68 characters per line, ink on chalk |
| Data | **Söhne Kräftig** · tabular lining figures | The number that matters, large; the identity's main emphasis device |
| Mono | **Söhne Mono Buch** | Product UI, code, identifiers only |

**Söhne Breit Kräftig** is permitted for a single word on a cover or fascia and nowhere else. Rules:
emphasis by weight and scale, never by colour · labels are the only caps · weight for emphasis, not
italics · never set YBMA in the typeface where it is the brand.

**Licensing — an operational note, separate from the design decision.** Söhne is licensed from
klim.co.nz per environment: desktop (by users), web (by domain, pageview tiers), app. Working
assumption: one desktop seat per founder and per designer; a web licence for ybma.ng and division
sites; an app licence when YBMA Technology ships a product. Outgoing documents go as PDF. Until the
licence is installed, Helvetica Neue stands in as an operational fallback only — nothing set in it
is final. Detail: `identity/brand/typography/TYPOGRAPHY.md`.

## 12 · Visual language

**One curved end, one square end.** The Y is square-top/round-bottom, the B square-left/round-right,
the M and A round-top/square-bottom. Photographs, cards, bars, tags and panels take the same shape —
a rectangle with one full semicircular end — in any of four orientations. The page is recognisably
YBMA without the logo.

- **Full semicircles only** — never small corner radii (product UI uses 4 px, deliberately not the
  brand shape). **One curve per element** — a pill is not a YBMA shape.
- **One round thing per composition** — the rest square. Without an accent this rule does the
  accent's job: the curve is where the emphasis lives.
- **The field** — one inverted panel (indigo or ink) per surface, carrying the one thing that matters.
- **The turn** — elements rotate a quarter-turn between states; motion is a cut or a quarter-turn.
- **Lines are stems** — 2 px, square-ended, ink; vertical more than horizontal; the system's
  underline, pull-quote mark and link style.
- **Grid** — 12 columns, 24 gutter, 8 baseline; module 3:4 (16:21).
- **Hierarchy** — label → headline → body → proof.
- **Data** — context bars grey and square-ended; the key bar indigo with the round end, the only one.
  Two series: indigo and ink. Never more than three fills.

## 13 · Photography

People doing things, photographed as they are. Documentary, close (35–50 mm feel), in whatever light
was there; true colour graded for contrast, not warmth; real, specific environments.
**Never:** stock boardrooms, pointing at laptops, flags, maps, kente/adire patterns, baobabs, sunsets,
"African lifestyle" polish, abstract technology imagery, posed teams on white.
**Treatments:** hero image always full colour — **the photograph is the accent** · indigo monotone for
secondary/background images (single ink, never a duotone) · crop square or with one round corner at
full height · **the cut** — a square photograph against an indigo field carrying the statement · no
gradient overlays; type sits beside images or on the field.

## 14 · Iconography

24-unit grid, 2-unit stroke (the wordmark's weight scaled), round joins, square ends, arcs of radius
3 (the 16:21 module at 24 px), ideally one curve per icon, ink or white only (indigo for a selected
state in product UI, never for the icon). Starting set of 12 in `identity/brand/icons/`; the set is
built as needed on these rules — no third-party library.

## 15 · Brand architecture

**One identity with descriptors. Divisions are named, not coloured.**

```
YBMA                       wordmark alone — always the group
└── YBMA Technology        wordmark + descriptor (first division)
    YBMA Real Estate       future, same rule
    YBMA Agriculture       future, same rule
    YBMA Financial Services  future, same rule
Acquired company           its own identity + endorsement (§16)
```

Re-tested (v0.2 and the stress test): all four divisions on chalk and reversed on indigo, one rule,
one colour — the parent identity is the common language. Descriptor: label style, caps, cap height
22% of the wordmark cap, 24 units below the baseline, left-aligned to the Y, same colour as the
wordmark; in a horizontal header it may sit right of the wordmark on the baseline after 40 units. No
division colours, symbols or typefaces. The parent is never a division; a division never uses the
bare wordmark. Products are named in text ("Ledger, by YBMA Technology").

## 16 · Endorsement

**A YBMA COMPANY** — symbol at 1.9× the text cap, text in label style standing on the symbol's
baseline, single colour. The wordmark is never used in the line.

| Entity | Carries | Endorsement |
|---|---|---|
| YBMA | wordmark · symbol · lockups | never — it is the endorser |
| YBMA Technology and divisions | wordmark + descriptor | never — the name says it |
| Acquired company | its own identity | **required** on: website footer/about · letterhead footer, proposals, contracts, profile · acquisition and appointment announcements · reception signage (secondary), packaging under the acquired name |
| Joint venture / partner | its own identity | by agreement — wording for the founders when it arises |

Omitted on anything YBMA or a division produces; in the acquired company's product UI (footer link
instead); in its advertising unless the relationship is the message; anywhere it would fall below
the symbol's minimum.

## 18 · Digital implementation

The website is an implementation of the identity, not a place to invent more of it. It consumes
`identity/brand/colour/tokens.css` (every colour, type, spacing, grid, shape, logo and motion
value), `identity/brand/web/ybma.css` (the component library — logo, label, display, heading, body,
proof, field, curved panels, crops, the cut, stem, rule, buttons, links, bars, division, endorsement,
nav, product status), `identity/logo/master/ybma-logo.js` (renders the logo in code) and
`identity/brand/web/WEB_FOUNDATION.md` (layout, responsive behaviour, navigation, accessibility).

**Rules the site keeps:** one round thing per view · one field per view; sections alternate chalk and
indigo, never two indigo adjacent, indigo and ink never meet · hierarchy label → headline → body →
proof, left-aligned, ≤ 68ch · ink buttons (one primary per view), underlined ink links, Indigo 60
focus rings · motion a cut or a 320 ms linear quarter-turn; no parallax, no scroll animation ·
product controls take the 4 px radius and the status colours, brand surfaces never do.

**Grid:** 12 columns · 24 px gutter · 32 px margin · 1240 max; 6 columns < 900 px; 4 < 560 px.
**Spacing:** 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 128. **Display** `clamp(44px, 7vw, 96px)`.
**Logo on the web:** header wordmark 22 px; footer horizontal lockup 28 px; favicon symbol ink on
transparent 16/32/48; app icon white on indigo 180/512. **Type:** Söhne self-hosted WOFF2,
`font-display: swap`, the fallback stack from `tokens.css`; nothing rendered in the fallback is final.

| Process | Wordmark (cap) | Symbol (side) | What sets the floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| Print, positive | 6 mm | 5 mm | guideline floors |
| Print, reversed | 8 mm | 6 mm | stroke as a knockout ≥ 0.35 mm |
| **Rubber stamp** | **8 mm** | **15 mm** | the joint (9.2 % of the side) closes on a worn die below 12 mm |
| **Embroidery** | **15 mm** | **25 mm** | the joint must hold ≥ 1 mm after fill pull; below 20 mm it fills in |
| Cut vinyl / acrylic | 20 mm | 25 mm | stroke ≥ 3 mm as a cut piece; joint ≥ 2 mm as a weed line |
| Signage read | 150 mm at 20 m · 40 mm at 3 m | 200 mm door · 60 mm plate | |

**Result of the desk test:** no mark fails at its guideline minimum in print. The symbol's joint is
the tightest feature in the identity and sets the stamp (15 mm) and embroidery (25 mm) floors; the
wordmark is more forgiving in every process. Nothing calls for a change to the geometry — where a mark
would fail, the rule is *size, or the other mark*.

**Process notes:** embroidery — satin for the wordmark up to ~8 mm stroke, fill above; symbol fill
with the stitch direction along the joint, 0.2 mm pull compensation, sample on the real fabric ·
stamp — deep-etch photopolymer from the vector PDF, wordmark 8–12 mm, symbol 15–20 mm, one ink ·
signage — flat-cut, no bevel, indigo fascia with white wordmark, ink door panel with white symbol,
face-lit only if illuminated · stationery — uncoated warm white 120–135 gsm; cards 350 gsm, solid
indigo back; folders 300 gsm indigo flood. **Bring to the proof:** indigo swatches 2758 C / 2758 U /
2748 U · wordmark at 6, 8, 12, 20 mm · symbol at 5, 10, 15, 25 mm · one stamp each · embroidered
wordmark 15 mm and symbol 25 mm · reversed wordmark 8 mm on indigo. Vendors receive PDF masters.

## 20 · Do and don't

**Do:** wordmark by default, lockup to introduce, symbol where the wordmark cannot go · one colour ·
two strokes of clear space · sentence-case headlines, caps labels · one round element per
composition · the one thing that matters in a field, or the largest thing on the page · people at
work, close, real light · descriptor for divisions, endorsement for acquisitions.
**Don't:** redraw, stretch, outline, shadow, gradient, rotate or recolour · set YBMA in a typeface ·
use the Y or any letter alone · add an accent colour, a gradient, a pattern, a division colour, or a
second indigo element on one surface · put indigo on ink · use small radii or pills as brand shapes ·
use U3 as decoration · flags, maps, kente, baobabs, sunsets, boardrooms · endorse YBMA's own or a
division's materials.

## Record — why there is no accent colour (from v0.2)

An accent is the easiest way to make a surface look energetic and the fastest way to look like every
other young company. Withholding one forces the energy into what is YBMA's — the mark, the shape
rule, the scale of the type, the photographs. The only job an accent did that nothing else could was
product state, solved by semantic UI colours outside the brand palette. Costs on record: the instant
read of "attention" is slower; feeds risk sameness unless the indigo–chalk–photograph cadence is
kept; a lazy three-colour application looks like a consultancy. The system depends on people using
scale, fields and crops — this guideline is the training.
