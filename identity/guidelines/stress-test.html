YBMA · Identity stress test · Session 27 · 14 September 2026

Ten situations, one system

Not mockups for their own sake. Ten completely different contexts built from the same components, three colours and one typeface, to show the identity stays coherent — including three divisions that do not yet exist and receive no colour of their own.

01 YBMA website hero

Chalk ground, wordmark in the bar, the headline as the largest element, the photograph with the one round corner, an underlined ink link and an ink button. No indigo on this surface at all — and it is still YBMA.

02 YBMA company profile

Full-bleed photograph with one round corner; the proof in the one indigo field; body below. Label → headline → body → proof.

03 Proposal cover

Indigo field, Halbfett display, labels, wordmark at the foot. The whole surface is the field.

04 Presentation cover

The ink ground — the night surface. Chalk type, white wordmark. Indigo is absent, which is allowed and useful: decks alternate ink, chalk and indigo.

05 Social post

Chalk: one number. The feed alternates indigo (a sentence), chalk (a number), photograph (colour).

06 YBMA Technology header

Descriptor beside the wordmark on the baseline; the hero splits chalk and an indigo field carrying the proof.

07 Technology dashboard

Chalk UI, 4 px product radius, ink text, indigo for the selected item and the one inverted tile, the key bar the only round-ended one, a desaturated status indicator. The brand is present without a single brand shape being misused.

08 YBMA Real Estate

Stacked descriptor lockup on chalk; the photograph (a site, a building under construction) with the one round corner; a proof block. No navy, no gold — the same three colours.

09 YBMA Agriculture

Indigo field with the descriptor lockup reversed; the cut — a square photograph of a farm (its green is the photograph's, not the palette's) beside the statement. No green anywhere in the identity.

10 YBMA Financial Services

A document: descriptor lockup, ink text, a stem holding the commitment, a proof number. The most conservative context, and the system needs nothing extra to look institutional.

Read

Coherent across all ten. The same four moves — scale, field, curve, stem — carry a marketing hero, a product dashboard and a financial document without a change of register, and the three future divisions are unmistakably the same company with nothing but a descriptor.

Where the discipline matters most: 01 and 10 have no indigo at all and depend entirely on typography and hierarchy — they are the surfaces a careless designer would "brighten". They must not be. The rule is a bigger number or an inverted field first, and colour never.
