# 1 · The approved logo, established as source of truth

**What was approved:** the mirror side-study, branch A, second pass — *one arch, four letters*.
Cap 100 · stroke 16 · one radius, 21 on the stroke centreline · tracking 12 · Y cup on a stem (58) ·
B two equal bowls lying (63) · M two arches standing (100) · A one arch over a bar (58) · **315 ×
100.** Shown in the approved artifact with **U3** at cap height.

**Production construction.** The artifact draws strokes; production outlines them. Because every
curve is a semicircle tangent to a straight, the outlines resolve exactly (outer radius 29, inner
13) — verified by overlaying the production geometry on the approved strokes: identical. **One
correction only:** the M and A crowns overshoot the cap by 1.2 units so round tops read level with
flat ones. Monoline weight deliberately uncorrected.

**Recorded, not changed:** the M is constructed as two arches (lowercase-m ancestry) and the
register of a rounded monoline is soft. Neither is a technical problem. **The system is built to
supply the seriousness the mark does not** — a hard grotesque, square-cornered layouts, ink text,
one deep ground colour, documentary photography.

**U3** carries forward unchanged as the symbol (approved S22–24; in the approved artifact). Its
relationship to the wordmark is **construction philosophy, not geometry** — the guideline says so and
claims no derivation. Confirmation listed for the founders.

# 3 · Why these colours (the decision that most needs the founders)

The brief excluded green, corporate blue, excessive red, gradients and "African premium" gold.
**Indigo** is Nigeria's dye colour (Kano, Abeokuta) and the world's denim — origin without motif,
deep enough for finance and property, violet enough not to be a bank. **Amber** is the one energetic
colour, used as a signal only, never metallic and never a background — which keeps it from being
gold. **Nothing else** is a brand colour: a second bright makes a startup palette. Neutrals are warm
so grey surfaces never go corporate-cold.
