# Interview 1 — Phone & Gadget Shop

**Business:** Mobile phones, accessories and repairs  
**City:** Kaduna  
**Staff:** 4  
**Sector:** Retail / Electronics

---

## 0 · CONTEXT

### What do you sell / do?

We sell phones, chargers, earpieces, power banks, smart watches and other accessories. We also do small repairs like screen replacement and changing charging ports.

### How many people work here including you? What does each do?

Four of us. I manage the shop and buy stock. Two people attend to customers and sales, and one person mainly handles repairs.

### How long running?

About six years.

### Customers or orders in a normal week?

Maybe 80 to 120 transactions in a normal week, depending on the period.

### More than one location? Vehicles?

Just this one shop for now.

---

# 1 · THE JOB

### "Walk me through yesterday. What did you actually do, hour by hour?"

I came around 8:30. First thing was checking the shop and seeing what was left from the previous day. Then customers started coming in.

Most of the day was sales, answering WhatsApp messages and checking prices from suppliers. I also had to call one supplier because some phones I wanted had increased in price.

Later I checked some of the accessories because one of the guys said we were running out of some chargers. In the evening we counted cash and checked transfers.

### What part of running this takes more time than it should?

Stock checking. Especially accessories. There are many small items and sometimes you have to physically look around before knowing what is available.

### What goes wrong most often?

Wrong stock figures and sometimes staff selling something without properly recording it.

### Last time something went wrong that cost you money — what happened?

We sold one phone and the staff member didn't record the correct model. When we checked later, the stock figure was wrong and we couldn't reconcile it properly.

### Roughly how much did that cost?

The immediate difference was around ₦45,000.

### What did you do about it? Did anything change after?

We checked the sales and spoke to the staff. Now I'm more strict about recording sales immediately.

### If you could hand one part of this business to someone tomorrow, which part? Why that one?

Stock management. If somebody could handle that properly and give me the correct figures, it would remove a lot of stress.

### Themes raised without being asked

- Stock
- Supplier prices
- Staff recording mistakes
- Cash reconciliation
- WhatsApp orders

---

# 2 · INFORMATION

### Without checking — how much stock of your best seller?

Probably around 12 pieces of the particular phone I sell most.

### Can you check?

Yes.

### How long did it take?

About five minutes because I had to check the physical stock and the record.

### How much did the business make last week? How do you know?

Sales were around ₦4.2 million. That's total sales, not profit.

I know because we check the sales records, transfers and cash.

### Walk me through how you work out whether you made a profit last month.

I take the total sales, then look at how much we bought the phones and accessories for. Then I remove expenses like salary, electricity, transport and other expenses.

But honestly, I don't calculate everything every day. I usually get a better picture at the end of the month.

### Who owes you money right now, how much?

Maybe two customers, around ₦85,000 altogether.

### Where does that information live?

Mostly WhatsApp conversations and my notebook.

### Last time one of those numbers surprised you?

Yes. We thought we had more profit from accessories than we actually did. When we checked, some of the margins were very small.

### Has money or stock ever gone missing without you noticing?

Stock has been difficult to reconcile before, yes.

### One number you'd want every morning without doing any work?

How much we made yesterday after expenses.

### Have you ever tried to fix this?

Yes. We tried using Excel and later a simple stock application.

### What happened?

Excel was too manual. The application wasn't really designed for the way we sell phones and accessories.

### Did you pay for anything to fix it?

Yes, I paid someone around ₦150,000 for the stock system.
