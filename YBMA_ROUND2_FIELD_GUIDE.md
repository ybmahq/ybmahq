## RULES — read before you start

1. **Ask about the last time, not the general case.**
2. **Ask to see things.** "Can you show me?" Observation beats testimony.
3. **Never say these words:** ownership · simplicity · capacity · visibility · standards ·
   quality · growth. If *they* say one first — **circle it and note the section.**
4. **Use silence.** The second answer is the real one.
5. **Turn every adjective into a number.** How many, how long, how much, how often.
6. **Money comes last.** Section 8, never earlier.
7. **You are not selling.** No pitching, no quoting, no deposits.
8. **Write their words, not your summary.**

**Say at the start:** *"I'm trying to understand how small businesses actually run day to day.
I'm not selling anything and I'm not quoting you for anything."*

## 2 · INFORMATION — 8 min

- Without checking — how much stock of your best seller? ______
  *(Then: can you check? How long did it take? ______)*
- How much did the business make last week? **How do you know?**
- **"Walk me through how you work out whether you made a profit last month — actually walk me
  through it."**
- Who owes you money right now, how much? How do you know?
- **□ SHOW ME where that information lives.** *(Photograph with permission.)*
- Last time one of those numbers surprised you? What happened?
- Has money or stock ever gone missing without you noticing? How did you find out?
- One number you'd want every morning without doing any work? ______
- **Have you ever tried to fix this? What did you try? What happened?**
- **Did you pay for anything to fix it? How much?** ₦______

*(The last two decide whether this is a problem or a complaint.)*

---

## 3 · WHAT THEY USE NOW — 5 min

- What do you use for records? **□ SHOW ME.**
- Who set it up — you, staff, family, someone paid?
- What does it do well? ______________________
- What does it **not** do? ______________________
- Has anyone suggested doing it differently?
- WhatsApp for business — for what exactly? *(orders / payments / records / catalogue / chat)*
- **Paying for any app or software right now, even small? How much a month?** ₦______
- Has a family member or staff member ever built or set up something for you?

**For each thing they use — ask:**
> **"What would have to happen before you'd stop using that?"**

## 4 · BRANCH — 12 min · **USE 4A *OR* 4B, NOT BOTH**

### □ 4A — HAS BOUGHT TECHNOLOGY

- Last technology you paid for — what was it?
- **"Take me back to before you bought it. What was happening in the business?"**
- **Was there a specific moment or event?** ______________________
- How long between that moment and paying? ______
- Who did you talk to? How many options?
- **How much did you pay?** ₦______
- Paid once / in parts / monthly?
- Why that provider, not the others?
- What did the others quote? ₦______
- What did you expect for the money? What did you actually get?
- **Still in use today?** □ Yes □ No — *if no: when did it stop, and why?*
- Who uses it — just you, or staff? How many? ______
- What did it change? Anything measurable?
- Was it worth the money? Why?
- Would you spend that again? Same thing or different?
- What would you do differently?

### □ 4B — HAS NEVER BOUGHT

- Has anyone tried to sell you software or a website? What happened?
- Ever come close to buying? **What stopped you?**
- **"Most you've ever spent on anything for the business that wasn't stock, rent, vehicles or
  salaries? What was it?"** ₦______ / ______________________
- Was it worth it? How did you decide?
- Know a business like yours that uses software? What do you make of their setup?
- If something existed that fixed *[their Section 1 problem, their words]* — what would have to be
  true before you'd pay?
- Who would you talk to before spending that kind of money?

## 5 · ACCESS & CONTROL — 7 min

### □ Has a website / system / paid software

- Website or online presence? **Whose name is the domain registered in?** ______________________
  *(Unsure? Do you know where it was bought? Do you have the login?)*
- Who pays the hosting — whose card or account?
- Do you have login details for everything? **□ CAN YOU SHOW ME YOU CAN LOG IN?**
- Who else has access?
- Customer list and records — where do they physically live? Could you move them tomorrow?
- **"If the person who built it stopped answering your calls tomorrow, what would happen? Walk me
  through the first week."**
- Has anything like that ever happened?
- **Ever changed developers or providers?** □ Yes □ No — *if yes:*
  - What made you change? ______________________
  - What did you rebuild or **pay for a second time**? ₦______
  - How long were you without it? ______
  - Did you get your data out? How?
- Did anyone explain what you'd own before you paid? **Did you ask?**

### □ No purchase history — platform version

- Business records — if your phone was stolen tomorrow, what would you lose?
- WhatsApp or Instagram business account locked tomorrow — what happens to the business?
- Has a platform ever changed something that hurt your business? What did you do?
- Customer contacts — where are they? Could you get them off that phone?

**THE THREE TESTS — tick what actually applies:**
□ Knows who holds domain/logins *(literacy only — weak)*
□ **Has asked / chosen / rejected / paid more / left a provider over access** *(BEHAVIOUR — strong)*
□ **Can quantify a cost from lock-in: ₦______ and ______ days** *(COST — strongest)*

## 6 · LEARNING & EFFORT — 6 min *(cut second if short on time)*

- Last app or system you learned — business or personal? ______________________
- How long before you were comfortable? ______
- What was hardest?
- Did someone teach you, or did you work it out?
- **Last app you stopped using? Why?** ______________________
- What parts of what you use now do you never touch?
- **Who here is worst with technology? What can they manage?** ______________________
- New staff member starts Monday — how long before they can use your records? ______
- Phone or computer for business things? Which for what?

**FORCED TRADE-OFF — read it out:**
> *"Two systems. One does exactly three things and nothing else. The other does twenty things but
> you only need three. Which would you rather have, and why?"*

Answer: □ three-things □ twenty-things — Why: ______________________

- What would make you call something "too complicated"? Example?

## 7 · BUSY PERIODS & PLANS — 5 min *(cut first if short on time)*

- Busiest week you've ever had — **what broke?**
- What did you do about it?
- If orders doubled next month, what fails first?
- Ever had to turn work away because you couldn't handle it?
- **Ever replaced something because the business got bigger?** What did the replacement cost vs
  what you first paid? ₦______ / ₦______
- Anything planned this year — new location, more staff, new product line?

**□ DO NOT ASK whether the plan changes what they need from their systems.**
**Did they connect it themselves?** □ Yes, unprompted □ No

## 8 · MONEY — 8 min · **LAST**

**Say first:** *"I'm not selling you anything and I'm not quoting you — I just want to understand
how businesses like yours think about this kind of money."*

### Behavioural

- **Largest single amount spent on the business in the last two years, other than stock, rent,
  vehicles, salaries?** ₦______ — What was it? ______________________
- Worth it? How did you decide?
- Who did you talk to before spending it?
- Anything monthly besides rent and salaries? ₦______

### The ladder — use THEIR problem, in THEIR words

> *"Suppose someone could reliably solve ___________________________ so that
> ___________________________. What would that be worth to you?"*

**Their figure first (do not anchor):** ₦______

| Price | Their reaction — exact words |
|---|---|
| **₦500,000** | |
| **₦1,000,000** | |
| **₦2,000,000** | |

**□ Mark where "expensive but maybe" becomes "not possible": ₦______**
*(The boundary is the finding, not the number.)*

- At ₦1,000,000 — what would you need to see or know before handing that over?
- Who else would have to agree before you spent ₦1m?

### Four trade-offs — ask all four

| | A | B | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ₦1m once | ₦100k/month for a year | | |
| 2 | **₦600k you fully own & control** | **₦300k provider keeps control** | | |
| 3 | ₦400k in three months | ₦800k in three weeks | | |
| 4 | ₦700k built only for you | ₦250k/year many businesses use | | |

## 9 · CLOSE + LANGUAGE TEST — 3 min

**Read all five. □ Rotate the order each interview.**

> 1. "We do digital transformation for small and medium businesses."
> 2. "We build software for businesses."
> 3. "We help you know what's actually happening in your business."
> 4. "We sort out your records, your stock and your money."
> 5. "We build the systems your business runs on."

- **Which would make you pick up the phone?** ______
- **Which means nothing to you?** ______

Then:
- Have you ever paid anyone **just for advice** about the business — no product, just advice?
- If you were advising me — what should a technology company **never** do to a business like yours?
- Anything I should have asked and didn't?
- Who else should I talk to?
